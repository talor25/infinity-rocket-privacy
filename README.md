# Privacy Policy for Infinity Rocket
**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026

Infinity Rocket ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide global leaderboard and country ranking features, the game generates an anonymous user identifier via Supabase Authentication and stores:
- Your chosen player nickname
- Your selected country flag
- Your maximum distance score

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, or payment details.

## 2. How We Use Information
The collected data is used strictly to display global rankings, calculate country competition scores, and maintain your game progress.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Leaderboard data is securely hosted on Supabase cloud servers using industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
